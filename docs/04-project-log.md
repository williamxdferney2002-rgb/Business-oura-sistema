## Lessons
1. **Replace first, automate later.** A manual app that retires the Excel is worth more than a brilliant bot on top of an old spreadsheet.
2. **AI never writes money on its own.** Preview + Confirm button + deterministic calculation. Zero discrepancies from "hallucinations".
3. **Free quotas fail at peak hours.** Design the model fallback chain and retries from day one, not later.
4. **A TEST copy with made-up data** makes testing on the phone safe; a sync tool with safeguards prevents pushing to the wrong copy.
5. **Measure speed in tests** (Sheet calls) instead of "feeling" it on the phone.
6. **If a security boundary cannot be demonstrated, it does not exist** (the orchestrator was postponed for that reason).
7. **The prompt is an index.** Copying context into prompts makes agents work on stale versions; pointing at the file does not.
8. **Correction rounds are normal.** Several tasks needed 4–6 rounds; a round costs far less than a bug in production.
9. **Human tests one at a time**, with what to look for and what to report. The owner does not need to read code.
